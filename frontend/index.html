Emotional well-being

AI That Notices and Cares

LifeConnect may gently notice changes in how you interact — and offer a kind word, your favourite music, or a prompt to connect with family. This is not medical monitoring. It's simply a caring companion.

🌻
You seem a little quiet today, Sharma Ji. Would you like me to call your son or play your favourite songs?

This AI can notice changes in interaction patterns and offer emotional well-being support. It does not diagnose or treat any medical or mental health condition.

Your privacy matters

Built on Trust and Safety

LifeConnect is designed with privacy at its core. You are always in control.

Private by Default

Your profile and memories are private. You choose exactly what to share and with whom.

Permission-Based Sharing

Family Bridge requires your explicit permission. Nothing is shared without your knowledge and approval.

Secure Memories

Your memory vault is accessible only to you. Future generations can only see what you choose to leave for them.

AI Transparency

Your AI companion is always clearly identified as AI. It does not pretend to be a person, and all its limitations are clearly stated.

Human Support

Behind the AI, there's a team of real people who care about your experience. Human support is always available.

You Are in Control

Delete your data, change your preferences, or leave anytime. Your memories belong to you.
